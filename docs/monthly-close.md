# Monthly operating close

## Square

- Export gross sales, discounts, refunds, sales tax, shipping income, processing fees, and net deposits.
- Reconcile every Square deposit to the business bank statement.
- Review inventory adjustments, stockouts, chargebacks, and failed payments.

## Soap Suite

- Close completed production batches.
- Confirm finished bars moved from cure status to available finished goods.
- Report raw-material, work-in-progress, and finished-goods ending values.
- Report cost of goods sold for the month.
- Reconcile available finished goods with Square sellable quantities.

## Accounting

- Record gross sales rather than treating net Square deposits as revenue.
- Record sales tax as a liability, not income.
- Record Square processing fees separately.
- Post shipping income and shipping expense separately.
- Post the Soap Suite inventory and cost-of-goods summary.
- Review the profit-and-loss statement, balance sheet, and sales-tax liability.

## Marketing and wholesale

- Record subscriber count, unsubscribes, and campaign results from MailerLite.
- Record site visits and top referrers from Cloudflare.
- Review Search Console indexing and search queries.
- Update each active wholesale prospect’s next action and date.
- Add software only when the recorded workload crosses a stated trigger.
