Cedar & Amber

Cedar Mesa

Cedar Mesa smells like the first shavings off a cedar board. Pine sharpens the dry wood scent, and amber, cinnamon and clove give it a warm, softly spiced finish.

Ingredients

Made with lard, olive oil, distilled water, coconut oil, sodium hydroxide (lye), castor oil, kaolin clay, fragrance (Cedar & Amber).

Between washes

Rest the bar on a draining dish so the underside dries between uses. Our Watershed dishes have channels that carry water away from the soap.

See the Watershed dishes

Using your soap

Work up a lather in wet hands and rinse well. For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes and discontinue use if irritation occurs.
