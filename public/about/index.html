Our story

Made where the desert meets the mountain.

Red Mountain Soap is a small production-minded soap business in Ivins, Utah—close enough to the cliffs that “red mountain” is a description before it is a brand.

Why six products

A line people can understand at a glance.

The opening collection is intentionally compact. Each bar needs a clear role, a distinct scent story, and a reason to remain in production.

That discipline matters behind the scenes, too. Small batches require planning around ingredients, molds, cure time, sellable inventory, and honest costs. The website should make the result feel simple because the operational work is not.

Brand compass

Rugged, refined, and restrained.

The visual language borrows from National Park posters, trail markers, old field guides, matte paper, and carved emblems—without turning Southern Utah into a costume.
